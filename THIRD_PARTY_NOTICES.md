## Eve chat template

The chat scrolling components in `src/modules/conversations/conversation.tsx` are adapted from the [Eve chat template](https://github.com/vercel/eve/tree/1cf022a6d2c096d30c62024dade8f093b456947e/apps/templates/eve-chat-template), published by Vercel under Apache License 2.0. Memoba changes the component primitives, transcript width, initial scrolling, and accessible names.

The composer and compact activity presentation also follow the template's interaction patterns. Memoba keeps its own structured request form, analysis events, source citations, authentication, and persistence. The Eve runtime is not included.
